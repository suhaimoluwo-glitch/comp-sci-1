About SnapPlastics

Hi, my name is Suhaim, and I run SnapPlastics — the world's most unexpectedly cool plastic-photography business. Plastic is everywhere: your phone case, your earbuds, your water bottle, that random toy you forgot you owned. But most people never actually look at it. That's where I come in.

I take photos that make plastic look sharp, dramatic, and sometimes weirdly beautiful. Businesses use my photos to show off their products. Teachers use them to explain recycling without putting everyone to sleep. Artists use them when they need inspiration but don't want to scroll for five hours.

My mission is simple: make people look at plastic and say, "Okay wait… that's kinda cool." I want to help people understand plastic better so we can reuse it, rethink it, and stop pretending it magically disappears. So if you ever need plastic photos that are crisp, creative, and not boring, SnapPlastics is your go-to.
